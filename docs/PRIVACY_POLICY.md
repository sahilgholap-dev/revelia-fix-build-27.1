## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information that you provide to us:

**Account Information:**
- Email address
- Name
- Password (encrypted)

**Birth Data:**
- Date of birth
- Time of birth (optional)
- Place of birth (optional)

**Social Login Information:**
- Apple ID (if you sign in with Apple)
- Google ID (if you sign in with Google)
- Profile information from social providers (name, email)

### 2.2 Biometric Data

**IMPORTANT:** Revelia collects biometric data in the form of facial and palm photographs.

**Face Photos:**
- Purpose: To analyze facial features and generate personalized face readings
- Collection: You voluntarily capture or upload face photos through the App
- Processing: Photos are analyzed by AI (Claude API by Anthropic) to identify facial characteristics
- Storage: Photos are stored securely in Cloudflare R2 cloud storage

**Palm Photos:**
- Purpose: To analyze palm lines and generate personalized palm readings
- Collection: You voluntarily capture or upload palm photos through the App
- Processing: Photos are analyzed by AI to identify palm lines and patterns
- Storage: Photos are stored securely in Cloudflare R2 cloud storage

**Your Rights:**
- You provide explicit consent before we access your camera
- You can delete your photos at any time through the App settings
- You can request deletion of all your data (see Section 8)

### 2.3 Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

**Device Information:**
- Device type and model
- Operating system and version
- Unique device identifiers
- Mobile network information

**App Usage:**
- Features used
- Reading history (types and dates)
- Time spent in the App
- Interaction with notifications

**Analytics Data:**
- App performance metrics
- Crash reports and error logs
- User engagement metrics

### 2.4 Location Data

We may collect approximate location data (city/country level) for:
- Birth chart calculations (if you provide birth location)
- Timezone detection
- Localized content delivery

We do NOT collect precise GPS location data.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Where We Store Your Data

**United States:**
- Primary data storage: MongoDB Atlas (US region)
- Image storage: Cloudflare R2 (global CDN)
- Backend servers: Railway (US region)

**Data Transfers:**
If you are located outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to this transfer.

### 5.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

**Encryption:**
- Data in transit: HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Data at rest: Encrypted database storage
- Passwords: Bcrypt hashing (never stored in plain text)

**Access Controls:**
- Role-based access to systems
- Multi-factor authentication for admin access
- Regular security audits

**Monitoring:**
- Real-time error monitoring (Sentry)
- Automated security scanning
- Regular vulnerability assessments

**Data Minimization:**
- We only collect data necessary for app functionality
- Automatic deletion of expired data (e.g., old insight caches)

### 5.3 Data Retention

**Active Accounts:**
- We retain your data as long as your account is active
- Photos and readings are stored indefinitely (until you delete them)

**Deleted Accounts:**
- Data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- Backups are purged within 90 days
- Some data may be retained for legal compliance (e.g., transaction records)

**Anonymized Data:**
- We may retain anonymized analytics data indefinitely
- This data cannot be linked back to you
