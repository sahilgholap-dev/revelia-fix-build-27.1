### Biometric Data Disclosure

**Important for App Store approval:**

```
Revelia collects face and palm photos for reading generation:

1. PURPOSE:
   - Face photos: Analyze facial features for personality insights
   - Palm photos: Analyze palm lines for life path readings
   - Both processed by AI (Claude API) to generate personalized readings

2. CONSENT:
   - Users explicitly consent before camera access
   - Clear explanation of why photos are needed
   - Users can skip or delete photos anytime

3. STORAGE:
   - Photos stored securely in Cloudflare R2 (encrypted)
   - Associated with user account
   - Deleted when user deletes account

4. SHARING:
   - Photos sent to Anthropic (Claude API) for processing
   - Not shared with any other third parties
   - Not used for advertising or tracking

5. RETENTION:
   - Stored as long as user has account
   - Deleted within 30 days of account deletion
   - Users can delete individual photos anytime

6. SECURITY:
   - Encrypted in transit (HTTPS)
   - Encrypted at rest (R2)
   - Access restricted to authorized systems only
```
